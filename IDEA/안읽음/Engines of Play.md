# Engines of Play: How Player Motivation Changes Over Time
**저자:** Jason VandenBerghe (Creative Director, Ubisoft)
**출처:** [슬라이드 PDF](https://chiplay.acm.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/CHI-PLAY-Keynote-2-VandenBerghe.pdf)
**분류:** 플레이어 심리 / 동기 설계
**발표:** GDC 2015

---

## 핵심 메시지

> "Play Engines: A clear expression of the essential motivations and satisfactions that your game will attempt to engage."

게임 디자이너는 자기 게임이 어떤 동기를 자극할지 선택해야 하고, 그것을 팀에게 의미 있는 방식으로 전달해야 한다. 세 가지 심리학 모델을 결합한 "Play Engine" 프레임워크를 제안한다.

---

## 주요 내용 (슬라이드 PDF 직접 기반)

### 1. 문제 정의
- "I make games. Games engage with people's motivations. People's motivations are hard to understand."
- 필요한 것: (1) 어떤 동기를 자극할지 선택, (2) 그 선택을 팀에게 전달
- 동기는 시간에 따라 변한다 / 장기 만족도가 기존 모델에서 빠져있다

### 2. 3개 모델 통합 ("Game Psychology Voltron")

슬라이드 원문:
> "Players motives and needs change in a predictable way over time — (Consumer's Journey)"
> "All players *start* to play for *different* reasons — (Big 5)"
> "But they tend to *stay* for the *same* reasons — (Self-Determination Theory)"

**모델 1: Big 5 → 5 Domains of Play**

| Big 5 (플레이어 성격) | 5 Domains of Play (게임 요소) |
|---|---|
| Openness to Experience | Novelty (신기함) |
| Conscientiousness | Challenge (도전) |
| Extraversion | Stimulation (자극) |
| Agreeableness | Harmony (조화) |
| Neuroticism | Threat |

각 도메인은 2개의 축으로 구성. 예: Novelty = Fantasy ↔ Realism, Builds ↔ Explores

**모델 2: SDT (Self-Determination Theory) → PENS 모델**
- **Competence**: "The universal want to seek to control outcomes and experience mastery" → Easy to learn, difficult to master
- **Autonomy**: "The universal urge to be causal agents of one's own life" → Customization, agency
- **Relatedness**: "The universal want to interact, be connected to, and experience caring for others" → Social grouping, status feedback systems

**모델 3: Consumer Journey (Forrester Research)**
- Discovery → Evaluation → Use → Affinity
- Discovery/Evaluation (획득 단계) = Big 5가 지배
- Use/Affinity (유지 단계) = SDT가 지배

### 3. 적용 예시 (슬라이드 원본)

**Destiny:**
| SDT | 게임 요소 |
|---|---|
| Competence | Core Shooting, PvP |
| Autonomy | Gear Hunting, Mission Loops |
| Relatedness | Strikes & Raids, PvP Ranking |

**The Stanley Parable:**
| SDT | 게임 요소 |
|---|---|
| Mastery | IT IS A LIE (illusion of challenge) |
| Autonomy | IT IS A LIE (illusion of freedom) |
| Relatedness | YOU ARE ALONE |

### 4. 주의사항 (슬라이드 원문)
- "This is a top-down technique."
- "It won't work well alone. Use with other, more bottom-up techniques."
- "Do not apply strictly. Stay loose."

- DDworld가 어떤 SDT 욕구를 자극할지 먼저 결정하기 (Competence/Autonomy/Relatedness)
